================================================================================
                           EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
                    CHURN ANALYSIS - STREAMING SERVICE
================================================================================

ANALYSIS DATE: 2024
DATASET: 500 streaming service subscribers
OVERALL CHURN RATE: 38.6% (193 churned / 307 retained)

================================================================================
KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

🎯 PRIMARY CHURN DRIVER: LOW USER ENGAGEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Users who churn show consistently LOW ENGAGEMENT across all metrics:
  • 20% LESS viewing time (66.6 vs 83.2 hours/month)
  • 20% FEWER binge-watching sessions (6.2 vs 7.7/month)
  • 18% FEWER unique titles watched (19.5 vs 23.7/month)
  • 14% SHORTER session duration (49.4 vs 57.8 minutes)

⚠️ CRITICAL INSIGHT: Users with <50 viewing hours/month have 60%+ churn rate


⚠️ STRONGEST WARNING SIGNAL: CUSTOMER SERVICE INTERACTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Churned users contact support 28% MORE than retained users (3.2 vs 2.5 times/year)

Churn rate escalates dramatically with CS interactions:
  • 0 interactions: 0% churn
  • 1-2 interactions: ~25% churn
  • 3-4 interactions: 40-57% churn  ⚠️
  • 5+ interactions: 62-75% churn   🚨

⚠️ CRITICAL INSIGHT: Users with 3+ support contacts are TWICE as likely to churn


💰 SUBSCRIPTION TIER MATTERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Significant difference in churn rates by tier (p=0.009):
  • Premium ($17.99): 24.1% churn ✓ LOWEST
  • Standard ($12.99): 39.5% churn
  • Basic ($7.99): 43.5% churn ⚠️ HIGHEST

⚠️ CRITICAL INSIGHT: Basic tier subscribers need urgent attention


================================================================================
STATISTICAL RIGOR
================================================================================

All findings are highly statistically significant (p < 0.05):
  ✓ Customer Service Interactions: p < 0.000001
  ✓ Total Viewing Hours: p < 0.000001
  ✓ Binge-Watching Sessions: p < 0.000001
  ✓ Unique Titles Watched: p < 0.000001
  ✓ Average Session Duration: p = 0.000001
  ✓ Subscription Tier: p = 0.009

Effect sizes range from SMALL to MEDIUM, indicating practical significance.


================================================================================
HIGH-RISK USER PROFILE
================================================================================

🚨 Users most likely to churn exhibit these characteristics:

  ✗ Viewing hours: <50 hours/month (60%+ churn rate)
  ✗ Customer service: 3+ interactions/year (40-75% churn rate)
  ✗ Binge sessions: <5 per month
  ✗ Unique titles: <15 per month
  ✗ Session duration: <45 minutes
  ✗ Subscription tier: Basic
  ✗ Genre preference: Horror or Thriller (niche content)

Combined risk factors create churn probability >60%
